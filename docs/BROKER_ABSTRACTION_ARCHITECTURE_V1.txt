PURPOSE:
Separate MarketCore platform identity from broker-specific integrations.

PRODUCT:
MarketCore

PRODUCT SUBTITLE:
Market Research & Analytics Platform

🧠 MarketCore

BROKER LAYER:
Broker integrations are adapters.

Current adapter:
- Finam

Future adapters:
- T-Bank
- QUIK
- Interactive Brokers
- Binance

RULE:
MarketCore is the platform.
Finam is a broker adapter.

UI STATUS BAR REQUIREMENTS:
The status bar must display:

- timezone selector
- currency selector
- broker selector
- current time

TIME FORMAT:
DD-MM-YY HH:MM

EXAMPLE:
TZ: Europe/Moscow | Currency: RUB | Broker: Finam | 08-07-26 03:42

DEFAULT VALUES:
timezone=Europe/Moscow
currency=RUB
broker=Finam

ARCHITECTURAL RULES:
- Product branding must use MarketCore.
- Broker-specific code may use Finam.
- FinamClient must remain broker-specific.
- Broker adapters must not affect Research Layer.
- Broker adapters must not be callable from UI directly.
- Execution remains disabled unless separately approved.
