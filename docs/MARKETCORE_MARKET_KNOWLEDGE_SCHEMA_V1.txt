MARKETCORE_MARKET_KNOWLEDGE_SCHEMA_V1

STATUS:
ARCHITECTURE_SCHEMA_DRAFT

GOAL:
Define the first ontology and PostgreSQL schema plan for MarketCore Market Knowledge Platform.

PURPOSE:
Create a broker-independent market knowledge layer that enriches Edge interpretation with market context.

IMPORTANT:
This stage defines ontology and schema plan only.
No execution.
No runtime mutation.
No Edge Score V2 formula change.

============================================================
1. CORE PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Market Knowledge is not a trading engine.

Market Knowledge does not send orders.

Market Knowledge does not change runtime.

Market Knowledge does not change execution.

Market Knowledge enriches interpretation of Edge.

============================================================
3. ENTITY DEFINITIONS
============================================================

Market:
A broad market domain, for example Russian market, US market, commodity market.

Exchange:
A trading venue, for example MOEX, SPB, NYSE, NASDAQ.

Asset Class:
Instrument group:
- equity
- futures
- fx
- bond
- commodity
- crypto
- index

Sector:
Economic sector:
- banks
- oil
- gas
- metals
- technology
- transport
- consumer
- utilities

Industry:
More detailed classification inside sector.

Instrument:
Tradable or observable market object:
- SBER@MISX
- LKOH@MISX
- BR
- NG
- USD/RUB

Market Regime:
Detected market state:
- trend_up
- trend_down
- range
- high_volatility
- low_volatility
- low_liquidity
- news
- holiday
- gap
- abnormal

Market Context:
Computed or observed context:
- volatility
- liquidity
- correlation
- spread
- volume
- atr
- session
- calendar
- macro
- sector_strength

Strategy:
Research strategy family:
- rsi_mean_reversion
- vwap_reversion
- bollinger_reversion
- breakout
- momentum
- conservative_breakout

Edge:
Research result linked to instrument, strategy and context.

Observation:
Shadow or research observation confirming, weakening or rejecting edge.

Recommendation:
Operator-facing interpretation:
- observe
- continue_research
- validate
- reject
- add_to_watchlist
- remove_from_watchlist

============================================================
4. SEMANTIC RELATIONSHIPS
============================================================

Market contains Exchange.

Exchange lists Instrument.

Instrument belongs_to Asset Class.

Instrument belongs_to Sector.

Sector contains Industry.

Instrument belongs_to Industry.

Instrument has Market Context.

Instrument is_in Market Regime.

Strategy works_best_in Market Regime.

Strategy weakens_in Market Regime.

Market Regime influences Edge.

Market Context explains Edge.

Instrument correlates_with Instrument.

Observation confirms Edge.

Observation weakens Edge.

Observation rejects Edge.

Recommendation is_based_on Edge.

Recommendation is_explained_by Market Context.

Edge is_enriched_by Market Knowledge.

8. DATA SOURCE RULES
============================================================

Market:
manual dictionary

Exchange:
manual dictionary

Asset Class:
manual dictionary

Sector:
manual dictionary

Industry:
manual dictionary

Instrument:
market data plus manual classification

Market Regime:
computed

Market Context:
computed from market data and analytics

Strategy Context:
computed from research and historical behavior

Edge Context:
bridge from Edge Score V2 to Market Knowledge

Relationship:
computed or manually curated with evidence

Observation:
from Shadow Observation and Research Platform

Recommendation:
from Explain Layer plus Market Knowledge

============================================================
9. VERSIONING
============================================================

Every table must include:

source_version

Important computed tables must include:

created_at

Optional for future:

knowledge_schema_version
knowledge_rule_version
knowledge_source_version

Versioning rule:
Knowledge rules may change without rewriting historical observations.

============================================================
10. SAFETY RULES
============================================================

Market Knowledge Platform must not:

- send orders
- place orders
- cancel orders
- execute orders
- call broker API
- modify runtime
- modify execution
- insert orders
- insert fills
- update Edge Score V2 formulas

Required invariants:

runtime_allowed=0
execution_allowed=0
micro_live_allowed=0
orders_changed=0
fills_changed=0

============================================================
11. EDGE SCORE V2 RELATION
============================================================

Edge Score V2 remains frozen.

Market Knowledge does not change Edge Score V2.

Market Knowledge creates additional interpretation layer:

Edge Score V2
↓
Market Context
↓
Edge Context
↓
Recommendation

Purpose:
Explain why a candidate is strong or weak under current market conditions.
